## A. Senaryoda kullanılan kaynaklar

### 1. ABD DOE: "The Harnessed Atom: Nuclear Energy & Electricity" (Student Edition, DOE/NE-0143)
- Adres: https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2014/06/f16/Student%20Edition_2.pdf
- Kullanıldığı yer: Sahne 1, Sahne 6, küçük resim 1, Shorts 3
- Alıntı:
  - "A uranium fuel pellet weighs less than 14 grams (0.5 ounce), which is less than an empty aluminum soft drink can. Each pellet can release as much energy as 477 liters (126 gallons) of oil, 1 metric ton (2,200 pounds) of coal, or 2.3 metric tons (5,000 pounds) of wood."
- Hesap: 1 metrik ton = 1.000.000 g; 1.000.000 ÷ 14 ≈ 71.429. Pelet 14 gramdan hafif olduğundan oran bundan büyüktür; senaryoda "yetmiş bin kattan fazla" denir.

### 3. ABD DOE (Office of Science): "DOE Explains...Nuclei"
- Adres: https://www.energy.gov/science/doe-explainsnuclei (sayfa notu: "This was last updated March 11, 2026.")
- Kullanıldığı yer: Sahne 2, Sahne 6, Shorts 1
- Alıntılar:
  - "Atomic nuclei consist of electrically positive protons and electrically neutral neutrons. These are held together by the strongest known fundamental force, called the strong force."
  - "The nucleus makes up much less than .01% of the volume of the atom, but typically contains more than 99.9% of the mass of the atom."
  - "The chemical properties of a substance are determined by the negatively charged electrons enshrouding the nucleus."
  - "The nucleus accounts for more than 99.9994% of the total atomic mass, but occupies less than one ten-trillionth of the atomic volume."
  - "All nuclei have approximately the same density. If the Moon was smashed to the same density, it would fit inside Yankee Stadium."
- Hesap (bezelye-stadyum benzetmesi): hacim oranı < 10⁻¹³ ⇒ çap oranı < (10⁻¹³)^(1/3) ≈ 4,6 × 10⁻⁵ ⇒ atom çapı > yaklaşık 21.500 × çekirdek çapı. 1 cm'lik bezelye için > yaklaşık 215 m. Seslendirmede sayı verilmez, yalnızca "stadyum büyüklüğünde" benzetmesi kullanılır.

### 4. ABD DOE (Office of Science): "DOE Explains...The Strong Force"
- Adres: https://www.energy.gov/science/doe-explainsthe-strong-force (sayfa notu: "This was last updated January 9, 2025.")
- Kullanıldığı yer: Sahne 3
- Alıntılar:
  - "At a larger level, the strong force holds protons and neutrons together to form atomic nuclei."
  - "In an atomic nucleus, the electromagnetic force should force positively charged protons apart because they have the same electric change. The strong force overcomes this effect."
  - "The strong force is about 100 times stronger than electromagnetism."
  - "The strong force only has influence over very, very small distances—it stops working when particles are even a tiny distance apart. How tiny? About 100,000 times smaller than the diameter of an atom."

### 5. ABD DOE: "DOE Fundamentals Handbook: Nuclear Physics and Reactor Theory, Volume 1 of 2" (DOE-HDBK-1019/1-93, Ocak 1993)
- Adres: https://www.energy.gov/documents/doe-hdbk-1019-93vol1
- Kullanıldığı yer: Sahne 2 (parçacık kütleleri), Sahne 3, Sahne 4, Sahne 5, Sahne 6
- Alıntılar:
  - Tablo 1: "Neutron Nucleus none 1.008665 amu / Proton Nucleus +1 1.007277 amu / Electron Shells around nucleus -1 0.0005486 amu"
  - "Comparing this result with the calculation of the gravitational force (10-24 newtons) shows that the gravitational force is so small that it can be neglected."
  - "Since stable atoms of neutrons and protons do exist, there must be another attractive force acting within the nucleus. This force is called the nuclear force."
  - "The nuclear force is a strong attractive force that is independent of charge. It acts equally only between pairs of neutrons, pairs of protons, or a neutron and a proton. The nuclear force has a very short range; it acts only over distances approximately equal to the diameter of the nucleus (10 -13 cm)."
  - "Careful measurements have shown that the mass of a particular atom is always slightly less than the sum of the masses of the individual neutrons, protons, and electrons of which the atom consists. The difference between the mass of the atom and the sum of the masses of its parts is called the mass defect (∆m)."
  - "Rounding off the masses of atoms and particles to three or four significant digits prior to the calculation will result in a calculated mass defect of zero."
  - "The BE/A curve reaches a maximum value of 8.79 MeV at A = 56 and decreases to about 7.6 MeV for A = 238."
  - "The nucleus is held together by very short-range attractive forces that exist between nucleons. On the other hand, the nucleus is being forced apart by long range repulsive electrostatic (coulomb) forces that exist between all the protons in the nucleus."
  - "To overcome this increased repulsion, the proportion of neutrons in the nucleus must increase to maintain stability."
  - "The BE/A of a nucleus is an indication of its degree of stability. Generally, the more stable nuclides have higher BE/A than the less stable ones."
  - "The increase in the BE/A as the atomic mass number decreases from 260 to 60 is the primary reason for the energy liberation in the fission process. In addition, the increase in the BE/A as the atomic mass number increases from 1 to 60 is the reason for the energy liberation in the fusion process, which is the opposite reaction of fission."
  - "The curve of binding energy per nucleon increases quickly through the light nuclides and reaches a maximum at a mass number of about 56. The curve decreases slowly for mass numbers greater than 60."
  - "Fission of heavy nuclides converts a small amount of mass into an enormous amount of energy."
  - Tablo 5 ("Binding Energies Calculated from Binding Energy per Nucleon Curve"): Rb-93 "8.7 MeV" × 93 = "809 MeV"; Cs-140 "8.4 MeV" × 140 = "1176 MeV"; U-235 "7.6 MeV" × 235 = "1786 MeV"; fark "199 MeV".
  - Kütle örneği: "Mass of Reactants 236.052589 amu", "Mass of Products 235.85208 amu", "0.200509 amu"; "EInst ... 186.8 MeV".
  - "The total energy released per fission will vary from the fission to the next depending on what fission products are formed, but the average total energy released per fission of uranium-235 with a thermal neutron is 200 MeV."
  - Belgede şekil listesinde "Combined Decay of Iron-56, Manganese-54, and Cobalt-60" (A = 56 çekirdeğinin demir olduğu, adıyla).
- Hesaplar:
  - 809 + 1176 − 1786 = 199 MeV (el kitabındaki sonuçla aynı).
  - Kütle kaybı oranı: 0,200509 ÷ 236,052589 ≈ 0,000849, yani yaklaşık %0,085 (binde birden az).
  - Proton/elektron: 1,007277 ÷ 0,0005486 ≈ 1836 (NRC'nin "1/1837" ifadesiyle uyumlu; senaryoda NRC ifadesi kullanıldı).
- Not: PDF'in metninde kütle farkı bir satırda "0.020059 amu" olarak da görünüyor; bu, aynı sayfadaki çıkarma işleminin sonucu (0,200509) ve 186,8 MeV ile tutarsız bir yazım hatası. Senaryoda bu değer kullanılmadı, yalnızca iki kütleden yapılan çıkarma kullanıldı.

### 6. ABD DOE: "DOE-HDBK-1122-99, Module 1.04 - Nuclear Physics, Study Guide" (Radiological Control Technician)
- Adres: https://www.energy.gov/documents/doe-hdbk-1122-99studyguidemodule104pdf
- Kullanıldığı yer: Sahne 4, Sahne 5
- Alıntılar:
  - "This equation expresses the equivalence of mass and energy, meaning that mass may be transformed to energy and vice versa."
  - "As we said earlier, the mass of an atom comes almost entirely from the nucleus. If a nucleus could be disassembled to its constituent parts, i.e., protons and neutrons, it would be found that the total mass of the atom is less than the sum of the masses of the individual protons and neutrons."
  - "The mass defect of 73Li is 0.042164 amu. This is the mass that is apparently "missing", but, in fact, has been converted to energy—the energy that binds the lithium atom nucleus together, or its binding energy. Binding energy is the energy equivalent of mass defect."
  - "1 amu = 931.478 MeV"
  - "The binding energy per nucleon peaks at about 8.5 MeV for mass numbers 40 - 120 and decreases to about 7.6 MeV per nucleon for uranium."
  - "On the average, approximately 200 MeV of energy is released per fission."
  - "Fusion occurs naturally in the sun and is the source of its energy."

### 7. ABD DOE: "DOE-STD-1128-98, Change Notice 1 (February 2005): Guide of Good Practices for Occupational Radiological Protection in Plutonium Facilities"
- Adres: https://www.energy.gov/documents/doe-std-1128-98cn1pdf
- Kullanıldığı yer: Sahne 6, Shorts 3
- Alıntı:
  - "Chemical reactions can release a few electron volts of energy per atom; however, when a plutonium nucleus splits, it releases about 200 MeV of energy and two or three neutrons."
- Hesap: 200 MeV = 200.000.000 eV. "Birkaç" (a few) en fazla 10 eV kabul edilirse 200.000.000 ÷ 10 = 20.000.000; 2 eV kabul edilirse 100.000.000. Senaryo yalnızca alt sınırı söyler: "en az on milyonlarca kat". Not: alıntıdaki 200 MeV plütonyum için; U-235 için ortalama 200 MeV değeri DOE-HDBK-1019/1-93'ten gelir.
